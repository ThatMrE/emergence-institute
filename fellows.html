Demonstrated activities

The dispatch log

DEF CON · Biohacking Village

Live red-team: screen before you print

Fellows ran the sequence-firewall hook against a benign test harness on the Village floor, then walked vendors through wiring in a screening call. Hazardous inputs were simulated, never real.

Community Lab · BSL-1

Detection-assay build night

An open bench session turning a fictional biomarker scenario into a working, non-hazardous detection panel — documented as a protocol any safety-reviewed lab can reproduce.

iGEM · Responsibility

Dual-use review clinic

A recurring clinic where fellows help student teams pressure-test their projects for misuse and build a disclosure plan before they publish.

Global Community Bio Summit

Tabletop: the outbreak that was printed

A facilitated cold-open drill that drops players into hour one of a synthetic outbreak, then races them to the countermeasure — all scenario, no recipe.

Vendor · Disclosure

Responsible-disclosure walkthrough

Fellows brought a screening-gap finding to a synthesis vendor first, with remediation time, before any public dispatch. The disclosure trail is part of the deliverable.

Publication · Red Horizon

Story + fix, shipped together

Each finished project runs as a paired release: the gonzo dispatch for the public, the working defense for the field, the hazard burned for everyone.
