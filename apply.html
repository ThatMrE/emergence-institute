Fourteen slots. Sixteen weeks, part-time, remote-first, ending in a live demo. No degree required — nerve and follow-through are. Writers, bench scientists, policy people, and red-teamers all belong here.

Who we take

You need to be strong in one of these and willing to learn the rest from your crew:

  • Writers — fiction, gonzo/immersive journalism, screenwriting.
  • Bench — wet-lab, synbio, diagnostics, hardware.
  • Policy — biosecurity, governance, science comms.
  • Red-team — security research, threat modeling, CTF.

Before you apply

Read the Safety Charter. If "publish the cure, redact the recipe" isn't something you can live by, this isn't the fellowship for you.

Do not send hazards

Your scenario pitch should describe a failure mode — never a method, sequence, or protocol for causing harm. Applications containing operational hazard detail are deleted unread.
